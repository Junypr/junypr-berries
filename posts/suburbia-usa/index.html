Suburbia, USA

Hello again, and again
the humid heat sticks to my skin
like the sap of square-cut rosemary bushes
too scared to exhale their scent
smile and nod.

How could four months do this to me?
a shadow among the fuzzy pinks and teals
empty, black
I walk too fast, talk too little
don't want a drink, don't want to go bowling
Good afternoon, how are you doing?

the only thing that lives here are the ducks
frantically paddling beneath the surface
I think anything bigger gets shot
What am I looking at?
Oh, just the hawk with the pigeon in its claws
The one you just startled
Guess you didn't notice

The birds sing regulation songs
for storybook families
in cages of regulation stucco walls
they scream in their sleep
we were like that once
Happy new year to you too.
is anything new?
